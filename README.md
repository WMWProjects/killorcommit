# Kill Or Commit

Three Active slots. Anything untouched for 21 days slides to The Bench, where you recommit with a dated next step or bury it with one line on what you learned. A weekly check-in shows what's on track, behind, blocked, and waiting on a decision. By Wholeminds Wellness.

**Install:** open https://wmwprojects.github.io/killorcommit/ in Google Chrome and click **Install App**. The app opens your private board in your Claude account, which stays in sync on every device.

## Privacy

- This repository holds only the app's launcher, icons and offline files. It never holds anyone's projects.
- Your board lives in your own Claude account, readable and changeable only by you.
